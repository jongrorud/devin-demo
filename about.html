The short version

I'm an elite enterprise software sales professional. For two decades I've helped category-defining technology companies win and expand their largest, most strategic enterprise customers — across AI, data platforms, cloud, analytics and enterprise SaaS.

I sell the hard, transformational categories before they're mainstream — Document AI at Eigen, big data and analytics at Cloudera (where I was the leading EMEA seller), FinOps at Apptio, and enterprise social at Yammer (acquired by Microsoft for $1.2B) — and translate them into outcomes the C-suite cares about.

The results have been consistent rather than lucky: President's Club in 7 of the last 10 years, multiple 130–300%+ years, and most recently Apptio Seller of the Year 2025 at 303% of target. I've also built and led EMEA sales teams as VP and Head of Sales — but winning and growing strategic accounts is the heart of what I do.
